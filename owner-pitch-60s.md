# The 60-Second Owner Pitch

Most builders lose owners because they start talking about agents, workflows and tools. Owners don't buy any of that. They pay so that things stop slipping.

## The script

> "Can I ask you something? When you're out on a job and the phone rings, what happens to that call?"
>
> *(Let them answer. Usually: "I miss it" or "I call back later.")*
>
> "And quotes you send out, does anyone follow up on the ones that go quiet?"
>
> *(Usually: "When I remember.")*
>
> "That's what I fix. I set up three things for your business.
>
> **A brain.** Everything about your business in one place: prices, customers, how you like to talk to people.
>
> **Staff.** Small AI assistants that each do one job. One texts back every missed call within a minute. One follows up every quote. One asks happy customers for a review.
>
> **A screen.** Every morning at 7 you get a text: what happened yesterday, what got handled, and the two or three things that actually need you.
>
> You don't learn any new software. You just read a text and reply if you want to."
>
> "If it catches one or two jobs a month you'd have lost, it's paid for itself. Want me to look at where things are slipping for you?"

## Why it works
- **Questions first.** They tell you the pain, so you don't have to claim it.
- **Brain · Staff · Screen** is three simple ideas in owner words. No jargon.
- **"You don't learn any new software"** answers the biggest objection before they raise it.
- **Ends on an easy yes:** a look at their business, which is your paid discovery.

## Words to drop
| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| AI agents | assistants / staff |
| workflow automation | it just handles it |
| knowledge base / RAG | the brain |
| dashboard | your screen / your morning text |
| integration | it plugs into what you already use |
