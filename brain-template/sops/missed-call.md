# SOP — Missed Call Text-Back
1. Within 60 seconds of a missed call, text: "Hi, it's Henderson Plumbing. Sorry we missed you, we're probably under a sink! What do you need help with?"
2. When they reply, find out: what's wrong, address/area, how urgent, best time.
3. If it's in the service area and a standard job → offer the next 2 open slots.
4. Emergency words (flooding, burst, no water, gas smell) → text Mike right away and tell the customer "Mike will call you in the next 10 minutes."
5. Log everything to `log/` and show it on the dashboard as "Calls caught".
