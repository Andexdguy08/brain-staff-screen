# SOP — When to Hand Off to a Human
Send straight to Mike (WhatsApp) and stop replying when:
- the customer is angry or mentions a refund, damage, insurance or legal action
- it's an emergency (water actively leaking, gas smell, no water)
- the price asked about isn't in `services-pricing.md`
- it's a commercial job over $1,000
- the AI isn't sure. **When in doubt, hand off.**
