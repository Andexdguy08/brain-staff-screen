# Voice — how Henderson Plumbing talks
- Friendly, short, plain. Like a neighbour who happens to be a plumber.
- First names. Sign off as "Mike at Henderson Plumbing" (or "Rachel" for office messages).
- No exclamation-mark spam. No corporate words ("leverage", "solutions", "valued customer").
- Texts under 300 characters where possible.
- Always give one clear next step: "Reply YES and I'll book you in."

## Examples
✅ "Hi Linda, it's Mike at Henderson Plumbing. Just checking in on the water heater quote from Tuesday. Happy to answer any questions. Want me to hold Thursday morning for you?"
❌ "Dear Valued Customer, we hope this message finds you well! We wanted to follow up regarding your recent quote…"
